One budget, two phones

An envelope budget my wife and I share: every dollar that comes in gets a job, spending comes out of envelopes, and we both see the same numbers as soon as either of us changes something.

Transactions arrive from our banks automatically and most file themselves by rules, so the day-to-day work is checking a short review list. One codebase runs as an Android app and as a website on iPhone.

What it does

  • Envelope budgeting. Income goes to Ready to assign, and you move it into envelopes. Balances carry over month to month, and an overspent envelope stays red until you cover it from another one, so the budget always matches the cash in the accounts.
  • Automatic bank sync. A scheduled server function pulls transactions through SimpleFIN, matches them against what's already there (including pending charges that post with new IDs), and keeps the bank credentials off the phones.
  • Rules and transfer matching. Rules file transactions by description, and transfers between accounts are paired automatically, including chains of same-amount moves across several accounts.
  • Splits, refunds and savings. Split a purchase across envelopes, send refunds back where they came from, and check that each savings account holds what its envelopes say.
  • Built for sharing. Supabase with row-level security keeps each household's data private, and live updates keep both phones in step. Over-the-air updates ship fixes without reinstalling.

The demo runs entirely in your browser with a made-up household: no sign-in, no bank connection, and nothing is saved. It uses the app's real screens and budget math, with an in-memory stand-in for the database. Changes reset when you reload.
