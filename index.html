Since November 2022 I've been a Project Coordinator for finance and market at Neighborly Development in Salem, Oregon, a real estate developer working mostly on market-rate multifamily projects, along with some affordable and mixed-affordability deals. I've delivered market and financial analysis on more than 10 projects totaling over $360 million and 1,000+ units, and I present that work directly to investors, lenders, and partners. Lately my work has shifted toward building internal tools, automating processes, and centralizing the company's data. One example is the Project Mapping Tool below, which should turn a site map that used to take an hour or more into a 10 to 15 minute job.

Before that, I maintained and extended Oregon's Driver and Vehicle Services software as an implementation consultant with FAST Enterprises on an Oregon Department of Transportation project, and built a developer dashboard in React and .NET Core as an intern at Restaurant365. I'm now working on a Master of Science in Analytics at Georgia Tech and looking for data science and analytics roles where a real estate finance background is an asset.
