Since November 2022 I've been a Project Coordinator for finance and market at Neighborly Development in Salem, Oregon, a real estate developer working mostly on market-rate multifamily projects, along with some affordable and mixed-affordability deals. I've modeled more than 10 deals (the largest a 264-unit multifamily project of roughly $60M) and I present that analysis directly to investors. Lately my work has shifted toward building internal tools, automating processes, and centralizing the company's data.

Before that, I maintained and extended Oregon's Driver and Vehicle Services software as an implementation consultant on an Oregon Department of Transportation project, and built a developer dashboard in React and .NET Core as an intern at Restaurant365. I'm now working on a Master of Science in Analytics at Georgia Tech and looking for data science and analytics roles where a real estate finance background is an asset.
